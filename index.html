Affirmative Case Construction

Key Vocabulary

Hover over the cards to reveal the detailed definitions.

Criterion

A helpful 'measuring stick' for the value; a uniform expectation to see how much of the value we are achieving.

Contention

A short, yet firm statement that directly relates and supports your position in the round, derived from your value and criterion.

Introduction

The beginning of the case structure (the 'hook'); a short introduction of the proposed ideology and a personal outlook on the resolution.

Value
Justification

The reasoning behind the choice of your value, expanding on its definition and linking it to the resolution.

Criterion
Justification

A few sentences to explain why the criterion is an appropriate measuring stick of the value, narrowing it down.

Case
Structure

A predetermined configuration that helps define and defend a debater's position, ensuring balance and focus.

Alternate
Case Structure

Varying ways to present a case structure that have their own unique focus but still successfully accomplish their objectives.

Flowing

A note-taking system that debaters use to follow argumentation throughout the round.

NC

Negative Constructive.

2AR

Second Affirmative Rebuttal.
